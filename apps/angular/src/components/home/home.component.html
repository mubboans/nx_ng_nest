MDX LAB Tech Whitepaper • 2026

The 800V Axial Flux Motor & Carbon Core Architecture

Authored by Chief Powertrain Architect • 5 min read

The MDX APEX VELOCITY GT utilizes dual direct-drive axial flux electric motors delivering 1,420 combined horsepower and 1,750 Nm of torque directly to the wheels without transmission friction losses.

Key Highlights:
  • Sub-2.0 second 0-100 km/h acceleration telemetry
  • Direct liquid immersion cell cooling with 800V DC peak charging
  • Torque vectoring adjusting individual wheel yaw 1,000 times/sec

By lowering the center of gravity and placing the cell modules in the monocoque floor pan, we achieve 50:50 weight distribution and exceptional high-speed lateral g-force stability.
